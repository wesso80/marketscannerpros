Implemented Journal quota repair
Under PUBLIC_DAILY_QUOTAS_ENABLED, verified non-bypass Free accounts can replace an above-five book if every retained open entry was already open in that same workspace. Entries can be closed gradually. A new, foreign, duplicate or reopened open entry above five is refused. At or below five, normal new entries remain permitted.
The quota decision happens on deduplicated input after reading authoritative IDs/open states while holding the existing journal quota advisory lock, before DELETE/INSERT. Existing paid, admin bypass and flag-off paths keep their prior quota behaviour. No stored record is automatically removed to bring an account under the limit.
Files: lib/free/journalQuota.ts and app/api/journal/route.ts. Other validation, risk governor, partial-replacement protection, lifecycle events and record persistence are unchanged.
