BEHAVIOR
New /api/research/m2-history authenticates and verifies paid subscription/admin
access before reading history. The redesign flag gates rollout. Supports one known
bloc and 12, 36 or 60 completed calendar months. SQL is a parameterized SELECT
against existing macro_series plus metadata; no provider calls or writes. Disabled,
signed-out, Free, invalid input, missing database and failed-query responses are
private/no-store. No shared response cache or account data is returned.

USD-millions storage values are converted to USD. Valid zero remains zero. Missing,
negative/nonfinite and duplicate-month observations are gaps, never interpolated.
The selected bloc retains its own coverage rather than summing changing sets of
blocs into a misleading historical global total. Observation month and ingestion
stamp are separate. Current metadata is explicitly not historical-vintage metadata.
The data is latest stored revisions, NOT the values known at a past date. No revision
backtest, forecast, model label, ranking or trading instruction is added.

UI
Pro/admin can open the history, choose bloc and 1/3/5 years, inspect a gap-preserving
chart and expand the date/source table. Data clears when selection changes; aborted
or superseded requests cannot repopulate an old selection. Free sees the history
upgrade link while retaining the complete current-summary trust information.
Pricing now lists stored M2 history by economic bloc under Pro.
