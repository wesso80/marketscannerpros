OUTCOME FINDINGS: REVIEW ONLY, SCORING UNCHANGED
1. Sample definition is latest 1000 non-null scores for workspace operator-terminal, not all signals and not a fixed calendar period. app/api/admin/model-diagnostics/route.ts:69–82. The three score selectors can therefore select different cohorts. Latest-signal truth stamp is queried separately and does not state the sample's oldest timestamp or latest label observation.
2. Hit rate is correct/(correct+wrong). Neutral, expired, pending and missing verdicts do not enter its denominator, although their valid-scored signals enter bucket.cases. lib/admin/modelDiagnostics.ts:52–77. Per-bucket labelled counts are computed but not returned. Thus the live high bucket's 25 signals is NOT the denominator of 36.4%. Exact database denominators were not retrieved and must not be reverse-engineered as established facts from rounded percentages.
3. The normal labeller uses price_at_signal and the first completed close at/after the 24-hour target. A >=1% move in the called direction is correct, <=-1% wrong; intervening paths and smaller moves do not establish a win/loss. lib/outcomes/aiOutcomeLabel.ts:23,61–90; app/api/cron/label-ai-outcomes/route.ts:140–177. This is not target-before-stop performance, realized portfolio P&L or performance after fees/slippage/funding. Daily fallback may extend effective observation beyond exactly 24 hours.
4. Old-method count is a separate all-history workspace count, while displayed rates use latest 1000 non-null-scored rows. app/api/admin/model-diagnostics/route.ts:84–89. The live 877 excluded verdicts should not be read as 877 exclusions from the displayed 1000-row sample.
5. Drift eligibility uses >=5 total signals in each bucket, not >=5 labelled outcomes. lib/admin/modelDiagnostics.ts:84. A bucket with many pending signals and one label can trigger a warning. Warning is a descriptive difference, not a statistical significance test or established model deterioration.
6. Pure helper excludes null/undefined/empty scores and preserves recorded zero. New regression tests confirm this. The live zero-score bucket therefore needs upstream row/version provenance; it is not explained by this helper coercing SQL NULL to zero. Current recorder saves Math.round(verdict.confidenceScore*100) into confluence_score and qualityScore into confidence (lib/admin/signal-recorder.ts:103–104). These names warrant explicit definitions; historical writers were not fully traced in this pass.
7. Fixed labeller cutoff is 2026-09-26T13:52:24Z. Old measurements are excluded based on outcome_measured_at. Date gating is useful but not a per-row labeller-version guarantee.
8. Current integer band endpoints would leave decimal scores between 24 and 25, etc., unbucketed. Current recorder rounds and elite selector rounds; investigate other/historical writers before expanding scope. No live fractional-score defect demonstrated.

SCANNER COVERAGE CAVEATS FOR THE NEXT AUDIT
The audit's findings infer survivorship bias from dormant-symbol counts. Dormant rows alone do not establish delisting coverage or point-in-time universe completeness; no dormant rows alone do not prove omitted delisted symbols. The completeness estimate uses 252/365 for non-crypto, not an actual exchange calendar, and yearsFor measures first-to-last span rather than uninterrupted history. These are screening heuristics, not proof a dataset supports an unbiased backtest. Also, every cg_hist_daily query failure is described as tables not created, even if the cause is permission/timeout. None of these behaviors is changed in the SQL patch.
